# Status, 2026-10-19 (update to 2026-10-17)

From this date the newest tested work is published on `master` of the three forks
(`MAXZVER/reims-vgpu`, `MAXZVER/qemu-reims-vgpu`, `MAXZVER/metal2vulkan`) instead of a
dated branch. `vendor/qemu` points at `master` of the QEMU fork; `Cargo.toml` still patches
`metal2vulkan` to a sibling checkout (`../metal2vulkan`, `master` of the fork). The
2026-10-16 and 2026-10-17 reports stay in the tree; where they differ, this one wins.

Same lab as before: Intel i7-14700K, RTX 4060, Windows 11 with WHPX, macOS Tahoe at
5120x2160 and 240 Hz, 24 vCPUs. Queue numbers (`bNNN`, `nNNN`) are our internal runs; each
queue alternates its arms, and only arms inside one queue are compared.

## Changed since 2026-10-17

- **Draw merging, on by default** (`REIMS_VGPU_LAB_MERGE_DRAWS=off` disables). The exec
  walk folds runs of small draws (Core Animation quads, map features) into one prepared
  record with sub-draws: each keeps its own index range, scissor and stencil reference, and
  a moved stage-in vertex buffer is rebound at an offset instead of re-staged. A page that
  draws thousands of small quads went from about 49 to 69-71 fps while dragged; Apple Maps
  +10-17 %. Sub-draws with their own device buffers stay capped at 8 per record
  (`REIMS_VGPU_LAB_MERGE_HEAVY`): more of them cost Maps fps.
- **`COMPOSITOR_FIRST`, on by default**: the presenting channel is drained first. Maps
  +3.6 to +10.5 fps in 4 of 4 alternated pairs; canvas and full-screen toggles neutral.
- **Chrome / Skia Graphite icons** (metal2vulkan): `air.render_target`'s second operand is
  the dual-source blend index (`[[color(0), index(1)]]`). It was dropped, so Dawn's coverage
  shaders put two outputs at Location 0, `spirv-val` refused the module and every draw with
  it was skipped. The second output now carries `Index 1` (Vulkan `dualSrcBlend` was already
  enabled) and those draws run. Chrome's toolbar and page icons are still missing with
  Graphite (see open problems); with `--disable-features=SkiaGraphite` they draw.
- **Whole-VM freezes of ~16 s on hosts without a microphone** (`vendor/qemu`,
  `audio/sdlaudio.c`): when a guest app opened audio input, virtio-sound opened the SDL
  capture device from a vCPU under the BQL, and WASAPI took ~8 s to refuse each of two
  tries. With no capture device the open now fails at once, and a failed one is not retried
  for a minute.
- **Crash at every QEMU exit with `QEMU_SLIRP_THREAD`** (`net/slirp.c`): the threaded
  slirp never registered the main-loop poll notifier, but cleanup removed it.
- **Crash and freeze diagnostics on Windows** (`os-win32.c`, WHPX vCPU watch): an unhandled
  exception prints the faulting thread's frames and every thread's frames (module+offset)
  and writes a minidump (`QEMU_CRASH_DUMP_DIR`); a vCPU out of the hypervisor for over 2 s
  dumps every thread's stack once per episode.
- **USB keyboard switch** (`hw/usb/host-libusb.c`): Ctrl+Alt+M on a passed-through keyboard
  hands that keyboard back to the host; the chord is armed only after M is seen released,
  so the press that sent the keyboard to the guest does not bounce it back.
- **Scaled present filters, opt-in**: FSR 1.0 EASU is the scaled present; RCAS sharpening
  (`REIMS_VGPU_LAB_PRESENT_RCAS`) and NVIDIA Image Scaling (`REIMS_VGPU_LAB_PRESENT_NIS`,
  vendored NIS SDK shader, MIT) are opt-in.
- **HiDPI (Retina) modes**: the guest already offers every published timing twice, at 1x and
  as HiDPI at half its size in points; the HiDPI ones are hidden from mode lists that do not
  ask for duplicate low-resolution modes. Opt-in lab switches: `REIMS_VGPU_LAB_DISPLAY_SCALE`
  (the display descriptor's scale-factor byte, published as `ParavirtDisplayPrefs`),
  `REIMS_VGPU_LAB_DISPLAY_EXTRA_MODES` (extra timings, e.g. 6912x2916 for a "looks like
  3456x1458" mode the host scales down) and `REIMS_VGPU_LAB_DISPLAY_MM`. A 2x mode at the
  panel's own size costs the same as 1x; larger backing sizes cost proportionally more.

## Known open problems

- Chrome with Skia Graphite (Dawn on Metal): toolbar and page icons do not draw, and text is
  sometimes drawn over older text; both go away with Graphite off. Not yet explained.
- Two crashes inside the NVIDIA Vulkan driver with a 6912x2916 HiDPI backing; not reproduced
  yet (the new crash stacks are meant to catch it).
- Safari video stops when switching Spaces (Chrome does not).
- 5K throughput is bound by guest-page dirty tracking and by the guest WindowServer's
  single-threaded Core Animation CPU on heavy pages.
